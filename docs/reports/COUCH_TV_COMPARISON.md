# Couch TV capabilities absent from Aurea Home

Compared Couch TV [main at b3d45ab](https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv/tree/b3d45ab) with Aurea Home testing at `36533c0`, before the account changes listed below. This is a source and feature-documentation review. Hardware support varies by Couch TV installation mode. Studio is a separate hosted product with private source.

| Area | Missing or narrower in Aurea |
|---|---|
| Adaptive wallpaper | Sun-position scene selection with a clock fallback, dawn/day/dusk/night variants, daylight color grading, Open-Meteo city and timezone selection, current conditions and forecasts, rain/snow/fog/storm overlays, weather-derived accents. Aurea has animated wallpapers and a separate music color treatment. |
| Other appearance options | Timed scene playlists, local/USB/web personal photos and rotation, video backgrounds/loop grades, OLED Cinema and idle photo modes, scene-depth clocks and reflections, adjustable clock placement/appearance, selectable icon sets and per-package icon/banner imports, customizable dock styling and size, optional focus sway and navigation sounds. Aurea already has application banners, HDMI customization and blur controls. |
| Keep Watching | Read-only Android Watch Next, app-published continuation channels such as Stremio, local video history from eligible app media sessions, per-TV/per-person shelves, focused-app filtering, YouTube-specific history, dismissal and reappearance after playback, progress and episode data when available, artwork enrichment, and Plex Continue Watching through the bridge. Netflix/YouTube exports seed earlier history and recommendations. |
| Show discovery and launch | Title and episode search with streaming availability, JustWatch lookup, supported app/title deep links, home-grown recommendations from viewing history, show requests and optional AI suggestions. Exact resume, autoplay and profile selection are app- and device-dependent. |
| People and kids | Watcher selection, profiles and combined viewing, per-person recommendations, bridge-driven selection of streaming-app profiles by configured names, exclusions for children and guests, and a curated kids screen with an app allowlist and parent PIN. Aurea's HDMI input profiles customize inputs and do not represent viewers. |
| Layout and widgets | Configurable start focus, hidden apps and app folders, remote app reordering and home settings, per-widget settings, custom JSON display cards, Studio design import/export by code and gallery. Studio's hosted editor is separate from the MIT repository. |
| Now Playing | Metadata and transport from general Android music sessions including Spotify when permission and installation path allow, full-screen player, Spotify queue and Home Assistant speaker controls. Aurea has AirPlay and paired Apple TV playback, cover colors and live activities. |
| Controls and idle | Configurable quick buttons, sleep timer and quiet hours, screen-off with audio, OLED idle clock and motion, idle or paused playback power-off, direct screensaver entry, soundscapes, automatic low-performance detection and 4K scene selection. Aurea already exposes inputs, several system settings and manual blur preferences. |
| Smart home | Home Assistant scenes, climate, doorbell/camera, energy and other sensor widgets. Optional bridge adapters for Matter, Hue and AirTouch, with feature support varying by adapter and device. |
| Household widgets | Clock styles, timers, world clocks, calendars, countdowns, moon/UV/air/surf widgets, photo displays and anniversary photos, agendas and booking greetings, family notes and lists, house guide, bin reminders, guest Wi-Fi QR, battery widgets and Android screen-time summaries. |
| Online widgets | RSS headlines, unofficial sports and markets feeds, estimated commute time and selected games/scores. Some providers use a relay, and some feeds can be disabled at the source. |
| Remote and multi-room | Show requests from the remote, match-aware cross-room playback handoff, approximate synchronized starts, and TV-side settings editing from another device. Aurea already has local/hosted pairing, multiple TV selection, keyboard, microphone, media keys and app/input launch. |
| Platform and setup | Couch TV has platform-specific app/bridge support across Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV and selected Samsung, LG, Roku, Apple TV, VIDAA, Philips, Panasonic, VIZIO and TiVo models. Some paths are remote-only, experimental or need an always-on bridge. It has guided desktop setup, compatibility checking and optional reversible privacy setup. Aurea is an Android TV home replacement with native phone/web remotes and can control a paired Apple TV. |
| Localization | Couch TV documents 16 languages and right-to-left support. Aurea Home documents English and Italian. |

## Fit for Aurea

1. Add an optional adaptive wallpaper in the existing wallpaper setting. Start with clock-based day phases and an opt-in manually selected city for forecast conditions. Use the current wallpaper component and suspend weather animation when Home is hidden.
2. Add an optional Keep Watching shelf using records supplied by participating apps. Show source and resume progress only when provided. Test access and deep links per Android TV model. Plex direct integration and local per-person history can be added independently. Playback metadata from unrelated apps may need separate permission or a supported integration. Existing Android TV and Apple TV controls do not expose general streaming watch history.
3. Add local household profiles and keep the active viewer, layout and viewing history separate. Make account linking optional. An app-level kids shelf cannot act as an OS-wide parental control.
4. Use X-Center ID to back up selected preferences, restore a TV on replacement, and carry an approved TV list to a new phone. An account claim can help discover a TV, while remote authorization should still require an approval on the TV and a new revocable credential for each remote. Keep hardware port mappings, watch history, IP addresses and third-party credentials local by default.
